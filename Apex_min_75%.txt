For production, generally no.

Salesforce requires:

At least 75% overall Apex code coverage for deployment to production.
All Apex tests must pass.
The 75% is overall org coverage, not necessarily 75% for every individual class.
Example
Class A → 90% coverage
Class B → 80%
Class C → 60%
----------------
Overall → 78% ✅

This can meet the overall 75% requirement.
